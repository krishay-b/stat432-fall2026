author: "Krishay Bugatha (bugatha2)"
---

Suppose we train three SVM models on the same dataset using linear, polynomial, and radial (RBF) kernels. How might their decision boundaries differ, and why might a more flexible kernel perform better on training data but worse on test data? How can cross-validation help us choose the appropriate kernel and its tuning parameters?
