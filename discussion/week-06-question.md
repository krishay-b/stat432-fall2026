A decision tree is grown until every terminal node contains only a small number of observations. The tree achieves very low training error but noticeably higher test error. Why might pruning some of the tree's splits improve test performance? Explain how removing splits affects the bias and variance of the model, and why a split that improves training accuracy may not be useful for predicting new observations.
