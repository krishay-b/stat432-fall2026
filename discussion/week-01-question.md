We have seen plenty of work on ways to train models, but I want to ask about training agents.

Is there a way to train an AI agent purely by having it learn from other data, or by watching a human perform the work? Instead of a person writing out the agent's skills by hand, the agent would observe the work being done and pick up the procedure itself.

If that worked, it would make the creation of skills largely obsolete. Agents would learn by themselves rather than depending on someone to author their instructions ahead of time.

I would like to discuss in class whether this is actually possible, and what it would take.
